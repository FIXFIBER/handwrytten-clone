How to Send Custom Holiday Cards Online

Category: Tutorials

Did you know 52% of consumers say they’d switch brands if they don’t see personalized content available? If you’re looking to keep your clients and leads happy without hassle or stress, look no further.

Send out custom holiday cards with a personalized, handwritten look this year. But not to worry! Personalized cards don’t equal extra work for you when you use Handwrytten. Instead of writing them yourself, robotic handwriting machines do the work for you. The results are nearly indistinguishable from handwriting.

With Handwrytten holiday cards, you have tons of options to choose from. You can upload bulk contact lists, and even automate your cards. You can even send out gift cards to show your gratitude. 

Handwrytten helps you deepen relationships seamlessly. Curious to learn more? Keep reading to learn how to send custom holiday cards online.

Handwrytten Holiday Cards for Clients, Friends, and Family

You may be wondering why holiday cards for businesses work so well. According to marketer Neil Patel, direct mail campaigns pack a serious punch.

Direct mail campaigns have a higher return on investment than paid search and online ads. Not only that, but sending mail creates a memorable connection with your clients, friends, and family.

Opening a letter has a nostalgic feel, and using realy handwriting adds a personalized touch. Compared to other forms of advertising, mail is less competitive and is sent right to the consumer. Your holiday message won’t get lost in a full inbox. Instead, you’re creating an opportunity to connect with your leads. 

Direct mail campaigns have a higher return on investment than paid search and online ads.

Personalize Your Holiday Message

Personalized content will keep your business top of your client’s minds. Unlike generic, impersonal content, your cards are written just for them.

When you’re coming up with holiday card messages, make sure you get clear on who your audience is. Use their name when writing their message or try to mention specific memories you share.

Come up with a short, respectful message. You don’t need to go too sales-y. The purpose of a greeting card is to foster a connection.

You can write about your gratitude for your work together and wish them a good year ahead. Also, keep in mind that not everyone celebrates the same holidays this time of year.

Instead of sending a funny Christmas card, you may want to send a holiday greeting. Stick with a neutral yet celebratory message for your card.
