Find the Best Holiday Card Message for Your Clients

It can be tough to find the right small business holiday message for your clients, but it doesn’t have to be. We recommend taking advantage of the provided messages offered by TOP10 NG. 

They’re simple, yet thoughtful, and show clients that you not only thought about them during this season of giving, but also crafted a unique personalized card just for them. If you’re still struggling to find TOP10 NG notes, we’ve also included some tips to narrow down your search.

How to Craft the Perfect Holiday Card Greeting

Think about the client and who you are as a company. This will help you select a message that will be well received by them. For example, if you’ve partnered with a joke shop during the last quarter, you might choose a funny holiday greeting that makes people laugh cheerfully when they open it.

If you want to send client cards, but aren’t sure which holidays they celebrate, there’s no need to worry! There are several ways to wish people a happy holiday season, without mentioning a specific holiday. Instead of saying “Merry Christmas“, consider “Happy Holidays” or “Wishing you a merry holiday season“.

Your message shows that you care and is a practical way to remain on their minds as you move into the new year. This is especially beneficial if you’re looking to retain the client and conduct business later on.

At TOP10 NG, we know it’s not just about the card, but what the card says. Don’t worry if you steal one of our examples. We won’t tell Santa.

Tips & Tricks to a Successful Holiday Campaign

It’s time to create the perfect holiday campaign, but there are some tips and tricks that can make things easier than you could’ve imagined. The first thing your company should do is start planning early.  This gives you the time you need to put your marketing plan to work without having to rush to get things done at the last minute. It also gives you a chance to make the most of the entire holiday season versus a few weeks before the new year.

Another recommendation is to create your own holiday hashtag and share it with clients and followers across social media. As you find different ways to increase customer communication, you’ll find that each of your posts gets more engagement. Encourage your followers to use your hashtag whenever they post photos using your products and services. Not only does this strengthen your online community, it also increases your online presence.

In 2019, more than $730.2 billion was spent on gifts around the world, meaning that whenever there’s an opportunity to win free items, people are going to take it. One of the best ways to ensure the success of a holiday campaign is to launch a contest.

Think about your ideal consumer and what they’d enjoy winning at the end of the content. Ensure you create a plan that allows you to spread information about it across all social media platforms effectively.

It’s also helpful to find a way to incorporate different interactive elements to increase contest engagement further.
